01 Annual Leave — Full-time employees receive 25 working days of annual leave per year. Leave requests must
be submitted at least 7 days in advance and approved by the reporting manager.
02 Remote Work — Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Remote work
requests must be submitted before the start of the working week.
03 IT Support — Employees experiencing technical issues must submit a ticket to the IT department. Critical
system outages should be reported immediately to the IT support team.
04 Expense Reimbursement — Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. All
reimbursement requests must include a valid receipt and manager approval.
05 Sick Leave — Employees must notify their manager when taking sick leave. A medical certificate is required
for absences exceeding 2 consecutive working days.
